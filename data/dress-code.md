# Dress Code

Our everyday dress code is business casual: collared shirts, blouses, chinos, dresses, or smart jeans without rips.

For client meetings and external events, step it up to business formal: suit or blazer, dress shirt, and closed shoes.

Fridays are casual: clean sneakers and plain t-shirts are fine, as long as you have no client-facing meetings that day.

What to avoid at all times: sportswear, flip-flops, shorts, and clothing with offensive prints.

If you are unsure what an event requires, ask the organizer or default to business casual. Nobody has ever been faulted for being slightly overdressed.
