# Sick Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days reset every January and do not carry over.

If you are unwell, notify your manager by 9:00 AM on the day of absence via chat or email. For absences longer than 3 consecutive working days, a doctor's note must be uploaded to the HR portal within 2 days of your return.

Sick leave is for your own illness or medical appointments that cannot be scheduled outside working hours. Up to 3 of your 10 days per year may be used to care for a sick dependent child or spouse.

Abuse of sick leave (for example, patterns of Monday/Friday absences without documentation) may trigger a review meeting with HR. Genuine health issues are always supported, and longer illnesses are covered separately under the long-term disability plan after 30 calendar days.
