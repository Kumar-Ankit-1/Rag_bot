# Travel Policy

Book all business travel through the company travel portal so trips are covered by corporate insurance.

Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours, business class for flights of 6 hours or more. Upgrades with personal miles are allowed as long as the ticketed class follows this rule.

Hotels: up to $180 per night in major cities, $140 elsewhere. Book cancellable rates whenever the price difference is under 15%.

Per diem: $60 per day for domestic trips, $90 per day for international trips. The per diem covers meals and incidentals; keep receipts for anything you claim on top of it.

Approval: trips must be approved by your manager before booking. Trips costing more than $3,000 need additional finance approval.

Bleisure: you may extend a business trip with personal days at your own cost. The company covers only the business portion of flights and hotels.
