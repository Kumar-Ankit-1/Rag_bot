# Working Hours Policy

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, including a 1-hour lunch break.

Core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Everyone is expected to be available and responsive during core hours, whether in the office or remote. Outside core hours you have flexibility: early birds can start at 8:00 AM, night owls can finish at 7:00 PM, as long as you work 8 hours per day.

Overtime is the exception, not the norm. If you regularly work more than 45 hours a week, talk to your manager about workload.

Weekend work is not expected. If an incident requires it, you get time off in lieu within the following two weeks.

Time tracking: you do not need to log hours daily, but you must record leave, sick days, and overtime in the HR portal.
