# Benefits Overview

Health insurance: the company covers 80% of the premium for a comprehensive plan including dental and vision. You can add dependents at your own cost during the annual enrollment window each November.

Pension: we match your pension contributions up to 5% of your base salary. Vesting is immediate.

Wellness: $50 per month gym subsidy, plus free access to the mental-health app for you and your household.

Time off: 20 days annual leave, 10 sick days, and all public holidays in your work location. See the leave policies for details.

Other perks: annual learning budget of $1,500, home-office stipend of $400, and a yearly company offsite.

Enrollment: new joiners are enrolled automatically in health and pension from day one. Adjust your elections in the HR portal within the first 30 days.
