# Acceptable Use Policy

Company devices and accounts are provided for work. Limited personal use is acceptable as long as it does not interfere with your duties or break any rule below.

Never allowed:
- Accessing, copying, or sharing production customer data for personal purposes.
- Installing unauthorized software or browser extensions on company devices.
- Disabling security tools such as antivirus, disk encryption, or the VPN.
- Using company systems for illegal activity, harassment, or running a side business on company time.

AI tools: you may use approved AI assistants for work tasks. Never paste customer personal data, credentials, or unreleased financial figures into any AI tool.

Personal devices: you may access email and chat from your phone via the approved apps with MFA enabled. Customer data must not be stored on personal devices.

Violations can lead to loss of access and disciplinary action up to termination.
