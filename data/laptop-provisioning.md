# Laptop Provisioning

New joiners receive their laptop on day one. The default device is a MacBook Pro 14 with 16 GB RAM and 512 GB storage. Developers can request 32 GB RAM with manager approval.

How to request: open a ticket in the ServiceDesk under "Hardware > New device" at least 5 business days before the needed date. For new joiners, IT provisions automatically from the HR start list, no ticket needed.

Replacements: laptops are refreshed every 3 years. If your device breaks, report it to the ServiceDesk; a loaner is provided within 1 business day while yours is repaired.

When you leave the company, return the laptop, charger, and any accessories to IT on your last day. Your accounts are deactivated the same day.

Accessories: one external monitor, keyboard, and mouse per employee are covered. Request them through the same ServiceDesk category.
