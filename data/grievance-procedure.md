# Grievance Procedure

If you experience unfair treatment, raise it. You can report a grievance to your manager, to HR, or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

File your grievance within 30 days of the incident. Describe what happened, when, who was involved, and what outcome you are looking for.

What happens next: HR acknowledges receipt within 3 business days and completes an investigation within 10 business days. You will be interviewed as part of the process, and so will the other parties involved.

You may bring a colleague or employee representative to any grievance meeting for support.

If you disagree with the outcome, you can appeal in writing within 10 business days. Appeals are reviewed by a panel that was not involved in the original decision.

Retaliation against anyone who files a grievance in good faith is a serious violation.
