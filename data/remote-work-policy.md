# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Traders operates a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The remaining days are expected in the office to support collaboration.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in your local timezone. You must be reachable on chat during these hours on remote days.

Working from abroad: with written approval from your manager and HR, you may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted 4 weeks in advance so payroll and tax implications can be checked. Work from countries under trade sanctions is never permitted.

Home office setup: the company provides a one-time stipend of $400 for remote equipment (monitor, chair, headset). Internet costs are not reimbursed.

Security: remote work must be done over the company VPN, and screens must be locked whenever you step away in shared spaces.
