# Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month from your start date.

Employees with more than 3 years of tenure earn one additional day per year of service, up to a maximum of 25 days.

How to request leave: submit your request in the HR portal at least 2 weeks before the start date for leaves of 5 days or more, and at least 3 business days ahead for shorter leaves. Your manager must approve the request before you book any travel.

Carry-over: you may carry up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Any days above that expire on December 31. Carried days must be used by March 31.

Leave during probation: new joiners in their 3-month probation period may take up to 5 days of accrued leave with manager approval.
