# Performance Review Process

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Every review covers the previous 6 months.

The process has three steps: (1) you write a self-assessment of your goals and achievements, (2) your manager adds their assessment and peer feedback, (3) you meet to agree on a rating from 1 to 5 and on goals for the next half.

Rating scale: 1 = below expectations, 3 = meets expectations, 5 = exceptional. A rating of 2 or below triggers a performance improvement plan with 90 days to recover.

Promotions: you become eligible for promotion after two consecutive ratings of 4 or above, provided there is an open role at the next level. Promotion decisions are confirmed in the December cycle and take effect in January.

Calibration: managers meet in calibration sessions to keep ratings fair across teams, so your rating reflects company-wide standards, not just your team's.
