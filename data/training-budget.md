# Training Budget Policy

Every employee has an annual training budget of $1,500 for courses, certifications, books, and conference tickets.

What qualifies: anything that builds skills relevant to your role or career growth, including online courses, professional certifications and their exam fees, technical books, and conference attendance (ticket only; travel follows the travel policy).

Approval: spending up to $500 needs your manager's approval in the learning portal. Spending above $500 needs manager plus HR approval.

Give back: after completing a course or attending a conference, share what you learned with your team in a 30-minute session or a written summary within 4 weeks.

Unused budget does not carry over to the next year. Certifications earned with this budget belong to you, but if you leave within 6 months of a certification costing over $1,000, a pro-rata repayment may apply.
