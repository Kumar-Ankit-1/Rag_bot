# Incident Response Policy

A security or production incident is any event that harms, or could harm, our systems, data, or customers: outages, breaches, malware, or data leaks.

Report immediately in the #incidents chat channel and page the on-call engineer. Do not try to fix a suspected breach alone; preserve evidence.

Severity levels and SLAs:
- Critical (customer data exposed or core service down): respond within 1 hour, resolve or mitigate within 4 hours.
- High (major feature broken): respond within 4 hours, resolve within 1 business day.
- Medium/Low: respond within 1 business day, resolve within 5 business days.

The incident commander coordinates the response and posts updates every 30 minutes for critical incidents.

Post-mortem: within 5 business days of resolution, the team writes a blameless post-mortem covering timeline, root cause, and preventive actions. Action items get owners and deadlines.
