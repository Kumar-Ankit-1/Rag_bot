# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Submit all expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of incurring them. Claims older than 30 days are rejected automatically.

Receipts are required for every expense above $25. For amounts below $25, a written explanation is sufficient.

Meal limits: client dinners are reimbursed up to $75 per person including tip. Internal team meals are reimbursed up to $40 per person. Alcohol is reimbursed only at client dinners, up to $20 per person.

Travel expenses follow the travel policy. Mileage for personal car use is reimbursed at $0.62 per mile.

Approval flow: your manager approves claims up to $500. Claims above $500 need additional finance approval. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

Fraudulent claims are a disciplinary matter and may lead to termination.
