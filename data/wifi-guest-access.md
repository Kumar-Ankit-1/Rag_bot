# Guest WiFi Access

Visitors can use the "GuestNet" wireless network in all offices. It provides internet access only; internal systems are not reachable from it.

How to connect: select the GuestNet SSID on your device. The password rotates daily and is displayed on the screen at reception. Ask the front desk if you cannot see it.

Sessions last 8 hours, then you are disconnected and must re-authenticate with the current password.

Employees: use the corporate "Northwind" network with your own credentials instead of GuestNet. Do not share your corporate credentials with visitors; point them to GuestNet.

Bandwidth on GuestNet is limited to 10 Mbps per device, which is enough for browsing and video calls but not for large downloads.
