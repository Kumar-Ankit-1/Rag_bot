# Data Retention Policy

We keep data only as long as we need it, then delete it securely.

Customer records: retained for 7 years after the end of the customer relationship, as required by financial regulations. After that they are anonymized.

Employee records: retained for 5 years after an employee leaves, then deleted, except payroll records which follow local tax law.

System and access logs: retained for 1 year, then automatically purged.

Backups: retained for 30 days on a rolling basis. Backup copies follow the same retention rules as the source data.

Email: mailboxes of departed employees are archived for 1 year, then deleted.

Deletion requests: customers can request deletion of their personal data at any time. The data team must complete verified requests within 30 days, unless a legal retention obligation applies.
