# Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to Northwind Traders! Your first two weeks are structured to get you productive fast.

Day 1: collect your laptop from IT, set up your accounts (email, chat, HR portal), and meet your onboarding buddy, who is your go-to person for everyday questions.

Week 1: complete the mandatory trainings in the learning portal: security awareness, code of conduct, and data privacy basics. Each takes about 45 minutes. Attend the company orientation session held every Monday at 10:00 AM.

Week 2: meet your stakeholders, get access to the team repositories and dashboards, and agree on your 30-60-90 day plan with your manager.

Your manager will schedule a check-in at the end of week 1 and week 2. If anything is blocking you, for example missing access or hardware, raise it with your buddy or in the #new-joiners channel immediately.
