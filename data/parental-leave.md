# Parental Leave Policy

Maternity leave: 16 weeks of fully paid leave, starting up to 2 weeks before the expected delivery date. An additional 8 weeks of unpaid leave can be requested.

Paternity and partner leave: 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first 6 months after birth or adoption.

Adoption leave: the same entitlements apply for adoptions of children under 6 years old.

Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start of leave so cover can be arranged. You are not required to share medical details beyond the expected date.

Return to work: you return to the same role or an equivalent one. A phased return (for example, 4 days per week for the first month) can be agreed with your manager.

Benefits, including health insurance and pension contributions, continue unchanged during paid parental leave.
