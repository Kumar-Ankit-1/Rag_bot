# Code of Conduct

We treat everyone with respect. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind is not tolerated and will be investigated.

Conflicts of interest: if you have a personal interest that could influence a business decision (for example, hiring a relative or selecting a vendor you have a stake in), disclose it to your manager and HR in writing before acting.

Gifts: you may accept business gifts up to $50 in value. Anything above that must be declined or donated, and reported to your manager.

Confidentiality: company and customer information must never be shared outside the company without written approval. This obligation continues after you leave.

Reporting: raise concerns with your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation and leads to disciplinary action.
